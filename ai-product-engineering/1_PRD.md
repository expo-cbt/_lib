# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Product Requirements Document** describes what the product should do and why.

It is written mainly by the Product Manager, and focuses on business goals, user needs, and features.

## Includes

- Product overview
- Objectives
- Features
- User stories
- Success metrics
